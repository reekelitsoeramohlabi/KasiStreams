About KasiStream

KasiStream is a student-designed video streaming platform created for the. Our mission is to showcase Southern African storytelling alongside STEM educational content and independent cinema.

Frequently Asked Questions

KasiStream uses the browser's built-in localStorage API to save title IDs locally on your device so they stay saved even after refreshing or closing the tab.
